Frequently Asked Questions

Are animations and transitions preserved in the PDF?

Animations and slide transitions are dynamic on-screen effects not supported by static PDF documents. Each slide is exported in its final state with all elements fully visible.

Can I export handouts or speaker notes?

Yes, select either "Slides with speaker notes" or "Handout" layout in the sidebar options to export slides with notes or 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, or 9 slides per page with ruled note lines.

Are my presentations uploaded to your servers securely?

Your presentation is sent over an encrypted TLS connection, converted in an isolated memory sandbox on our server, and deleted automatically after 10 minutes.

Can I convert password-protected presentations?

Password-protected presentations should have their encryption removed before uploading so our conversion engine can parse slide elements.
