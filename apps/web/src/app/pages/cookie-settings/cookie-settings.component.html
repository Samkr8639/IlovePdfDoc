STORAGE PRIMITIVE

Browser LocalStorage

LocalStorage stores key-value pairs purely inside your device's browser sandbox. Unlike traditional HTTP cookies, LocalStorage data is never automatically attached to outgoing HTTP requests, reducing server overhead and eliminating unintended header leakage.

HTTP COOKIES

First-Party HTTP Cookies

Cookies are small strings transmitted in request headers. PDF flow limits cookie usage strictly to optional translation helpers (googtrans). When functional cookies are rejected, existing translation cookies are explicitly expired and deleted.

ZERO ADVERTISING

No Third-Party Trackers

Unlike many commercial PDF services, PDF flow does not embed Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, Google AdSense, or affiliate marketing scripts. Your document processing workflows remain confidential and free of ad tracking.

BROWSER-LEVEL INDEPENDENCE

Managing cookies in your browser settings.

In addition to our on-page consent controls, all modern web browsers allow you to block, audit, or delete cookies and site data globally or per domain.

🌐

Google Chrome

Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → Manage site permissions or clear browsing data for pdfflow.app.

🦊

Mozilla Firefox

Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data.

🧭

Apple Safari

Preferences / Settings → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking → Manage Website Data → Remove pdfflow.app.

🌀

Microsoft Edge

Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data → See all cookies and site data.

Private / Incognito Browsing: When you use private browsing windows, all cookies, LocalStorage keys, and session tokens created during your visit are automatically and permanently purged as soon as you close all private tabs.
