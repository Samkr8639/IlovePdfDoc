Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to any server?

No. PDFTailor runs 100% locally in your web browser. Your pictures and personal documents are never transmitted across the internet, ensuring complete confidentiality.

Which image formats are supported?

You can convert JPG/JPEG, PNG (including transparency), WebP, GIF (first frame), and BMP images. TIFF and HEIC should be converted to JPG before adding.

Does converting images to PDF reduce photo quality?

By default, the "Original" setting preserves the exact original pixels and DCT quantization tables with zero compression loss. You can also select "Optimised" or "Small file" to reduce file size.

Can I convert each image into a separate PDF?

Yes! Uncheck "Merge all images into one PDF" in the sidebar options, and PDFTailor will package individual PDFs for each photo into a convenient ZIP archive.
