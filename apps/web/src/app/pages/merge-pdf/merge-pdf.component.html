Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files uploaded to your servers when merging?

No. PDFTailor operates with 100% in-browser WebAssembly. Your documents remain strictly inside your device's memory and are never transmitted across any network.

Can I merge PDF files with different page orientations?

Yes! PDFTailor automatically detects and preserves portrait and landscape orientations, margins, embedded fonts, and page dimensions across all files.

How many files can I merge at once?

You can combine multiple PDF documents in a single operation. Because processing happens locally on your computer, performance is fast and unrestricted.

Can I open files directly from Google Drive or Dropbox?

Yes, simply click the Google Drive or Dropbox shortcut buttons next to the Select Files button to open your cloud storage in one click.
