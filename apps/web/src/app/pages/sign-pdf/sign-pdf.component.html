Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a simple electronic signature or a certificate-based digital signature?

Sign PDF places simple electronic signatures visually embedded in the PDF. It does not issue qualified or Aadhaar eSign certificates.

What happens if my PDF already contains a digital signature?

We detect existing cryptographic signature dictionaries on load and warn you before editing, since saving new annotations invalidates prior signatures.

Can I save my signature for future documents?

Optionally tick "Save this signature on this device" to store it locally in IndexedDB only. You can delete individual items or clear all anytime.
