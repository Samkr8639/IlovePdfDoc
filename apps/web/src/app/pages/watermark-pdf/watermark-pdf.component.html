Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about stamping watermarks with PDFTailor.

Is my PDF document uploaded to any external server?

No. PDFTailor operates 100% locally inside your web browser memory. Your files never touch external servers or cloud storage.

Can I watermark only specific pages in my PDF?

Yes! Under the Pages section, select "Custom range" and enter page numbers or ranges (e.g. 1-3, 5, 8-10).

Will adding a watermark damage my existing PDF forms or links?

No. Our export engine isolates the graphics state and preserves existing AcroForm fields, hyperlinks, bookmarks, and metadata.
