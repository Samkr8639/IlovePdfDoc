PRODUCT PURPOSE

Document confidentiality without software friction.

Traditional online PDF converters require users to upload sensitive personal or legal documents to opaque cloud servers, frequently accompanied by mandatory signups, paywalls, and cross-site tracking scripts.

PDF flow changes this paradigm: by running document manipulations directly in the user’s browser sandbox via WebAssembly and limiting cloud processing to ephemeral, RAM-only execution with immediate automated file wipes, PDF flow delivers full-featured document workflows while keeping user data private and confidential.

INTEGRITY & USAGE STANDARDS

Brand usage guidelines.

To maintain consistent brand representation, please adhere to these official presentation standards when referencing PDF flow.

Clear Space Requirements

Maintain a minimum clear margin around all sides of the logo equal to at least 25% of the symbol's total height. Do not crowd the logo with headlines, navigation bars, or other icons.

Minimum Display Sizing

To preserve legibility and fine lines, the primary horizontal logo must never be displayed smaller than 120 pixels wide (32 mm for print). The standalone icon mark must not be smaller than 24 × 24 pixels.

Official Typography

Our primary display headlines utilize Space Grotesk (Bold/SemiBold). Interface labels, body paragraphs, and descriptive text use Plus Jakarta Sans. Code and technical tokens use monospaced fonts.

Incorrect Logo Treatments

Please avoid the following modifications to our brand assets:

×
Do not stretch or distort Always maintain standard aspect ratios when scaling vector or PNG assets.
×
Do not alter brand colors Do not replace the coral gradient or slate typography with unauthorized colors.
×
Do not add heavy shadows Do not apply dramatic drop shadows, neon glow filters, or 3D bevel effects.
×
Do not crowd with background patterns Do not superimpose the primary dark logo onto complex, high-contrast imagery.
