Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Convert pages and Extract images?

Convert pages renders every PDF page into a complete, high-resolution JPG image preserving all text, layout, and formatting. Extract images extracts only the embedded photos and illustrations in their native resolution.

Are my PDF files uploaded to any server?

No. PDF to JPG runs 100% locally in your browser memory using WebAssembly. Your documents never leave your device.

Can I customize image resolution and quality?

Yes! Choose from 72 DPI (web) up to 1200 DPI (print quality) with customizable JPEG quality presets.
