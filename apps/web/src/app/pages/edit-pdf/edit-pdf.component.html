Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files uploaded to your servers when editing?

No. PDF editing runs completely client-side in your browser memory using WebAssembly. Your files are never uploaded to any remote server during editing.

Can I edit existing text in a PDF document?

Yes! Simply click the "Edit Text" toggle in the top bar. The editor recognizes text blocks on your page and lets you update words, fix typos, and change styling in-place.

Can I sign and fill PDF forms?

Yes. You can create cursive or drawn signatures, place dates and text boxes, and insert whiteout or shapes anywhere on the document.
