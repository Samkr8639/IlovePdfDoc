Frequently Asked Questions

Are my spreadsheets kept private and secure?

Yes. Your documents are transmitted over encrypted TLS connections, processed in isolated volatile sandboxes, and automatically deleted from our servers after 10 minutes. We never store or inspect your data.

Will wide tables or columns be cut off in the PDF?

No. With our smart scaling options (such as "Fit all columns on one page"), all columns are scaled proportionally to fit the page width, allowing rows to flow naturally across pages without truncating tables.

Can I convert multiple sheets or multiple files at once?

Yes! You can choose to convert all visible sheets, select specific sheets from your workbook, or upload up to 10 spreadsheet files simultaneously and download them individually in a ZIP or merged into a single PDF.

Are calculated formulas like =SUM() preserved accurately?

Yes, all calculated numbers, dates, and cell values are preserved exactly as displayed in Excel.
